Automatic Plant Watering System
===============================

## About the Project

This project was completed by a team of three students for our Exploration in ECE course. We wanted to build something simple that could solve a real-life problem, so we decided to make an automatic plant watering system.

The idea is pretty straightforward. A soil moisture sensor checks if the plant's soil is dry. If it is, the Arduino turns on a small water pump to water the plant. Once the soil has enough moisture, the pump shuts off. This way, the plant gets water only when it needs it.

## Parts We Used

- SunFounder
- Water Pump
- Relay Module
- Breadboard
- Jumper Wires
- 5V battery
- Water Container

## How It Works

1. The moisture sensor checks the soil.
2. The SunFounder reads the sensor value.
3. If the soil is too dry, the SunFounder turns on the water pump.
4. The pump waters the plant.
5. Once the soil reaches the right moisture level, the pump turns off.
6. The system keeps checking the soil and repeats the process whenever the plant needs water.
